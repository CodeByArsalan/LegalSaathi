# Legal Document Template Definitions

This directory stores structural definitions and clause markup for Legal Saathi templates across the 4 core categories:

1. **Personal & Family**
   - General Affidavit (Bayan-e-Halfi)
   - Lost CNIC / Degree Affidavit
   - Character & Residence Certificate
   - General Power of Attorney (Mukhtar Nama Aam)
   - Special Power of Attorney (Mukhtar Nama Khas)

2. **Real Estate & Tenancy**
   - Residential Rent Agreement (Bilingual English/Urdu)
   - Commercial Lease Agreement
   - Property Bayana / Token Sale Agreement
   - Tenancy Vacation & Eviction Notice

3. **Business, Freelance & SME**
   - Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
   - Freelancer Independent Contractor Agreement
   - Full-Time / Part-Time Employment Agreement
   - Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
   - Partnership Deed (Sharakat Nama)

4. **Vehicles & Assets**
   - Motor Vehicle / Motorcycle Sale Deed & Delivery Receipt
   - Promissory Note & Debt Acknowledgment (Iqrar Nama)

These definitions will be seeded into SQL Server database tables (`Templates` and `FormFields`).
